Terms of Service & Privacy Policy — Last updated: 2026-09-14

AtlasPoster is a personal, single-user automation tool. It prepares video content and delivers it to the developer's own TikTok inbox using TikTok's Content Posting API. It does not automatically publish anything: the developer manually adds sound and posts each video themselves from within TikTok.
